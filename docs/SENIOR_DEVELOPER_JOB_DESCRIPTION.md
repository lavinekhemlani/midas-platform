# Senior Full-Stack Developer - Midas AI Financial Platform

## About Midas

Midas is an AI-powered financial management platform that's reimagining how businesses interact with their financial data. We're building the next generation of financial intelligence by combining multi-provider accounting integrations with advanced AI capabilities to deliver real-time business insights through natural conversation.

Our platform connects directly to QuickBooks, Zoho Books, and other accounting systems, transforming passive financial data into an active AI CFO that provides specific recommendations, predictive analytics, and automated financial advisory services. We're competing against industry giants like Intuit by being faster, more innovative, and laser-focused on user experience.

## The Opportunity

We're a lean, ambitious team of 3 developers (CTO + 2 developers) taking on a massive market opportunity. The AI revolution in financial services is just beginning - 71% of CFOs aren't using AI yet. We're positioned to capture this market with our conversational AI approach that goes beyond traditional automation to provide genuine financial intelligence.

This role offers the unique opportunity to:

- Shape the technical direction of a product competing against $150B+ companies
- Build cutting-edge AI systems using the latest LLM technologies
- Own significant portions of the codebase with direct impact on users
- Work with a modern tech stack at the intersection of fintech and AI

## The Role

As our Senior Full-Stack Developer, you'll be a technical leader who can both architect systems and write production code. You'll own critical features end-to-end, mentor junior developers, and help establish the engineering culture that will scale us from startup to industry leader.

### Key Responsibilities

**Technical Leadership (40%)**

- Architect and implement complex features across our multi-agent AI system
- Lead the development of our LangChain-based AI orchestration layer
- Design scalable solutions for real-time financial data processing
- Establish engineering best practices and code quality standards
- Drive technical decisions on architecture, tools, and frameworks

**Full-Stack Development (40%)**

- Build and optimize React/Next.js frontend components with exceptional UX
- Develop robust API endpoints with proper error handling and rate limiting
- Implement complex financial calculations and KPI engines
- Integrate with external APIs (QuickBooks, Zoho, banking providers)
- Optimize performance across the stack (sub-200ms response times)

**AI/ML Engineering (20%)**

- Enhance our multi-agent system with specialized financial advisors
- Implement RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) for financial context
- Optimize prompt engineering and LLM token usage
- Build intelligent memory and conversation management systems
- Create dynamic visualization generation from natural language
