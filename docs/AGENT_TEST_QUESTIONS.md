## Expected Behaviors to Verify

For Each Query, Check:

### 1. **Specificity (Drill-Down Principle)**

- ✅ Mentions specific account names
- ✅ Provides dollar amounts AND percentages
- ✅ Lists line-item details
- ❌ NO vague statements like "and other assets"

### 2. **Proactive Fetching**

- ✅ Analytical queries fetch breakdowns automatically
- ✅ Ratio queries fetch asset + liability breakdowns
- ✅ Comprehensive queries fetch multiple datasets in parallel

### 3. **Follow-Up Detection**

- ✅ Detects follow-ups with explicit keywords ("that", "it")
- ✅ Detects topic-related follow-ups (assets after balance sheet)
- ✅ Uses cached data (no redundant API calls)

### 4. **Recommendations**

- ✅ Tied to specific accounts/invoices
- ✅ Include dollar amounts and impact calculations
- ✅ Actionable (not generic advice)

### 5. **Context References**

- ✅ References previous queries: "Based on the balance sheet we discussed..."
- ✅ Builds on conversation history
- ✅ Works across logout/login sessions
